IPSWICH TOWN FC - MEDIA REPLACEMENT GUIDE
=============================================================

Instructions:
1. Place all your new image/video files into the `src/assets/` folder.
2. Ensure you use the EXACT FILE NAMES (without extensions) listed below.
3. Any of the following file formats will be accepted automatically: 
   .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .jfif, .webp, .svg, .mp4, .webm
4. Delete the old default images when you place your new ones to avoid having duplicate files.

NOTE: The following files exist in src/assets/ but are NOT used by the site:
  - logo-dark.png  (unused — safe to delete)
  - logo-light.png (unused — safe to delete)
  - video-thumb-hutchinson.jpg (old file, replaced by video-thumb-nunez — safe to delete)
NOTE: The club badge/crest shown in the Navbar and Footer is loaded from an 
  external URL (Wikipedia) and does NOT need a local file.
